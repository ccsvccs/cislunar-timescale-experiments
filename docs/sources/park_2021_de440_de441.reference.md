## Reading guide

1. Sec. 2.3, Eq. (3)–(7): TT-to-TDB integrand, geocenter specialization, scalar/oblateness/vector/higher-PN terms, sign and time argument.
2. Sec. 3: origin of ephemeris states, dynamic model and modeled perturbing objects; separate *orbit perturbations* from *explicit potential contributions to time-scale integration*.
3. Section on observations/fits: ephemeris source uncertainty vs quadrature stability.
4. Compare against Turyshev Eq. (21) in `docs/model_audit.md`; no implied numerical identity from superficially similar formulas.
